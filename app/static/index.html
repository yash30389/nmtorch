Interpret results cautiously

  • Person IDs are anonymous, temporary tracking labels—not identity recognition. They reset when monitoring stops.
  • Arm/leg movement labels describe visible landmark changes only. Walking/running labels are rough heuristics. Facial expressions and emotions are not inferred; this is not a fall detector, safety alarm, or medical tool.
  • Occupancy and dwell time describe only what this camera currently sees. No calibrated zones, cross-camera tracking, or congestion claims are made.
  • Models can miss or mislabel people, poses, and objects. Keep monitoring consensual and stop it when no longer needed.
